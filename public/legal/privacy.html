1. What we use from your ServiceM8 account

When you approve {{NAME}} in ServiceM8, it may read and write only what it needs to work:

WhatWhy
Jobs: job number, address, status, category, description/notes, badges and billing linesTo show each job's costs, suggest which job an invoice belongs to, and work out the job's price and profit

Job diary files and notesTo bring invoice files in from the diary when you choose to, and to add invoice PDFs and short notes (and, if you switch it on, a summary note) to the diary

We do not read or use your customers' contact details, payments, quotes or invoices you send to customers except where they appear in job notes or diary files you choose to bring in.

2. What you give us

  • Supplier and subcontractor invoices you upload, forward, or that arrive in a mailbox you connect, and the details read from them (supplier, invoice number, dates, lines, amounts).
  • Mailbox access, if you connect one. Outlook (Microsoft 365) is connected through Microsoft's sign-in; we keep the access token Microsoft gives us, never your Microsoft password. For Gmail or another mailbox you give us an app password, which we store encrypted. We only read your mailbox: we never send, change, move or delete emails. Emails that aren't bills are not kept.
  • Price lists, rules, supplier statements and settings you add.
  • Which invoices are paid, notes, and how costs are shared between jobs.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you use {{NAME}}. If you disconnect (Settings → Disconnect) we stop using your ServiceM8 account and mailboxes; your invoices stay so you don't lose them if you come back. Email {{EMAIL}} to have everything deleted: we delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records. We keep usage counts (how many invoices were read each month) for billing.

5. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Mailbox passwords are stored encrypted. Each business only sees its own information. Access to the servers is limited to the people who run {{NAME}}.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export (Bills → Export everything) or delete your information, and you can complain to us at {{EMAIL}}. If you're not happy with our answer you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). We handle personal information under the Australian Privacy Principles.
